Connect | Empower | Succeed

The Sikh Professionals Network connects UK-based professionals, students, and community leaders to foster career excellence, leadership, and pride in their heritage. Rooted in integrity and seva, this trusted group empowers its members to succeed at work while actively serving those in need.

Features List

Connect

Build connections with Sikh professionals from different industries, cities and career levels. SPN creates opportunities for members to meet, share job opportunities, learn from each other and help the generation.

Empower

As a member, you will gain access to talks, resources, mentorship, career guidance and support that recognises the importance of your faith. This will help you grow spiritually, professionally and personally.

Succeed

Develop the skills, network and mindset to advance your career and make a meaningful contribution to society. At SPN, success means striving for excellence in your profession, taking pride in who you are, and serving others with purpose.

Common questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know about joining and getting the most from SPN.

Who can join SPN?

SPN is open to Sikh professionals, students, and graduates aged 18+ who are based in the United Kingdom. Whether you are just starting your career or are an established professional, you are welcome to join.

Do I need to be established in my career to join?

No. SPN welcomes members at all career stages — from students and recent graduates to experienced professionals. Whether you are just starting out or looking to give back through mentoring, there is a place for you.

What does SPN offer members?

Members benefit from professional networking, mentorship and career support, faith and identity guidance, events and talks, practical resources and toolkits, and opportunities to volunteer and serve the community.

Is SPN only for one profession or industry?

No — SPN brings together professionals from a wide range of fields including law, finance, medicine, technology, education, business, public service, engineering, and the charity sector.

How can I get involved as a mentor or advisor?

If you are an experienced professional who would like to mentor or advise SPN members, please get in touch via hello@sikhpn.org. We are always looking for knowledgeable volunteers to share their experience and guide the next generation.
