Data Protection Complaint

Last Updated: August 2026

Submit a Data Protection Complaint

At Sikh Professionals Network (SPN), we are committed to protecting personal information and respecting the data protection rights of our members, users, participants, and their families.

If you believe we have not handled your personal data appropriately, or your data protection rights have not been respected, you can submit a formal Data Protection Complaint using the process below.

We are committed to investigating every complaint fairly, confidentially, and without unnecessary delay.

This form is intended solely for data protection and privacy-related matters. It should not be used for general service enquiries, programme enquiries, event-related complaints, or other matters unrelated to the processing of personal data.

Your Right to Complain

You can use this form to submit a complaint to us about how we have handled your personal data or your data protection rights.

We take our obligations under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018) seriously and welcome the opportunity to resolve any concerns.

When we refer to personal data, we mean any information that can directly or indirectly identify you or another living individual.

If you have concerns about how Sikh Professionals Network (SPN) processes personal information relating to you or your family, you may submit a formal complaint using this page.

Complaint Process & Timescales

Once we receive your complaint, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt of your complaint.
  • Review the information provided.
  • Assess the nature of your data protection concern.
  • Contact you if additional information or identity verification is required.
  • Investigate the matter fairly, appropriately, and confidentially.
  • Provide a written response outlining the outcome of our review.

We aim to review and respond to all Data Protection Complaints within 30 calendar days.

Where necessary, we may request additional information from you before completing our investigation.

How We Use Your Information

The personal information submitted through this complaint process will be used solely for investigating and responding to your Data Protection Complaint.

Your information may be used to:

  • Investigate and respond to your complaint.
  • Communicate with you regarding your concern.
  • Take appropriate action where necessary.
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Your information will only be shared where:

  • You have provided your consent;
  • It is necessary to investigate your complaint;
  • We are legally required to disclose the information; or
  • Disclosure is otherwise permitted or required under applicable law.

For more information about how SPN collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

If You Are Not Satisfied

If you remain dissatisfied after we have completed our investigation, you have the right to submit a complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent authority responsible for upholding information rights and data protection legislation.
