Why Join?

Joining SPN connects you with a community of Sikh professionals across the UK who share your ambitions and values. Members gain access to mentoring, networking opportunities, and leadership development, alongside chances to give back through seva. You'll find practical resources, meaningful events, and a supportive network committed to helping you grow in your career while staying grounded in your identity.

FAQ

Answers to common questions about becoming a member.

What happens after I submit the form?

Once you submit the form, a member of the SPN team will review your details and be in touch by email. We'll share next steps, upcoming events, and ways to get involved based on the interests you've shared.

Is there a membership fee?

SPN is committed to remaining accessible to the community. There is no cost to register your interest and join the network. We'll confirm any details about specific events or programmes when we contact you.

Will my details be shared publicly?

No. The information you provide is kept private and used only by the SPN team to understand your background and how best to support you. We never share your details publicly or with third parties without your consent.

Can I join if I'm a student?

Yes. SPN welcomes students aged 18 and over, alongside graduates, early-career professionals, and experienced leaders. Whatever stage you're at, there's a place for you in the network.

Can I join as a mentor or advisor?

Absolutely. If you'd like to support others by mentoring, speaking, or volunteering, simply indicate this on the form. We're always looking for experienced professionals to help guide and inspire the community.
