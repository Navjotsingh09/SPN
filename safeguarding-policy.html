Safeguarding Policy

1. Our Commitment to Safeguarding

Sikh Professionals Network ("SPN"), owned and operated by Devanhaar, is committed to creating a safe, inclusive, respectful, and supportive environment for everyone who engages with our community, programmes, events, mentoring initiatives, workshops, and digital platforms.

We recognise our legal and moral responsibility to safeguard and promote the welfare of children, young people, and vulnerable adults. Every safeguarding concern is taken seriously and handled promptly, fairly, and confidentially in accordance with applicable safeguarding legislation and best practice guidance.

We are committed to:

  • Protecting children, young people, and vulnerable adults from abuse, neglect, exploitation, discrimination, and harm.
  • Promoting a culture of openness, accountability, and respect.
  • Responding promptly and appropriately to safeguarding concerns.
  • Working alongside parents, schools, partner organisations, and statutory authorities where appropriate.
  • Regularly reviewing this policy to reflect legislative, regulatory, and operational changes.

2. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to everyone participating in or representing Sikh Professionals Network, whether through online or in-person activities.

3. Safer Recruitment & DBS Checks

SPN is committed to safer recruitment practices to help protect children, young people, and vulnerable adults. Where roles involve regulated activity or direct engagement with children or vulnerable participants, we may require:

  • Identity verification.
  • Appropriate references.
  • Enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) checks where legally required.
  • Safeguarding awareness or role-specific safeguarding training.
  • Agreement to comply with SPN's safeguarding policies, procedures, and Code of Conduct.

No individual will undertake regulated activity on behalf of SPN until the appropriate safeguarding requirements have been completed where applicable.

4. Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)

SPN has appointed a Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) responsible for overseeing safeguarding matters and ensuring concerns are managed appropriately.

The Designated Safeguarding Lead is responsible for:

  • Receiving and reviewing safeguarding concerns.
  • Maintaining confidential safeguarding records.
  • Providing safeguarding guidance to staff, volunteers, and mentors.
  • Liaising with statutory safeguarding authorities where required.
  • Reviewing safeguarding procedures and supporting continuous improvement.

Safeguarding Contact
Email: contact@devanhaar.com

If someone is at immediate risk of harm, contact the emergency services by calling 999 before contacting SPN.

5. Responsibilities of Staff, Volunteers & Mentors

Everyone representing SPN is expected to:

  • Treat all individuals with dignity, fairness, and respect.
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries.
  • Complete safeguarding training where required.
  • Report safeguarding concerns immediately.
  • Promote a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment.
  • Follow all safeguarding policies and procedures.

No individual should ignore, delay, or attempt to resolve a safeguarding concern without following the appropriate reporting process.

6. Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in SPN activities is expected to behave respectfully, professionally, and responsibly. All participants, members, staff, volunteers, mentors, and speakers must:

  • Treat others with dignity and respect.
  • Use appropriate language and behaviour at all times.
  • Respect privacy and confidentiality.
  • Follow event rules, safeguarding guidance, and organisational policies.
  • Avoid bullying, harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or abusive behaviour.
  • Never engage in conduct that may place another individual at risk.

SPN reserves the right to remove or restrict participation where behaviour compromises the safety, wellbeing, or dignity of others.

7. Digital Safety & Communication Rules

SPN recognises that safeguarding extends to digital environments. Official communications relating to SPN activities should only take place using authorised communication channels.

  • Personal social media accounts should not be used for official programme communications.
  • Personal contact information should not be exchanged unless authorised.
  • Online meetings and mentoring sessions should be conducted using approved platforms.
  • Personal information should only be shared where necessary and in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
  • All online interactions must remain respectful, professional, and appropriate.

8. Two-Adult Communication Rule

Where reasonably practicable, communications involving participants under the age of 18 should follow the Two-Adult Rule. This means:

  • At least two authorised adults should be included in communications where appropriate.
  • Group communications should be used instead of private messaging wherever possible.
  • Parents or legal guardians may be copied into communications relating to minors where appropriate.
  • One-to-one communication with children should only occur where necessary for safeguarding or programme administration purposes.

This approach promotes transparency and helps safeguard both participants and representatives of SPN.

9. Allegations Against Staff, Volunteers or Mentors

Any allegation involving a member of staff, volunteer, mentor, trustee, contractor, speaker, or representative will be treated seriously.

  • Record and assess the concern.
  • Temporarily restrict involvement while the matter is reviewed.
  • Refer the matter to the Designated Safeguarding Lead.
  • Notify safeguarding authorities or the police where required.
  • Cooperate fully with external investigations.

Confidentiality will be maintained wherever possible while ensuring safeguarding responsibilities are fulfilled.

10. Reporting Safeguarding Concerns

If you believe a child, young person, or vulnerable adult may be at risk of harm, you should report your concern immediately.

Our safeguarding process includes:

  1. Reporting the concern without delay.
  2. Recording the concern securely and accurately.
  3. Assessment by the Designated Safeguarding Lead.
  4. Taking appropriate safeguarding action.
  5. Referring the matter to statutory safeguarding authorities where required.

If there is an immediate risk of serious harm, contact 999 before notifying SPN.

11. Security & Data Protection

SPN is committed to protecting personal information through appropriate technical and organisational security measures. These include:

  • Secure systems and encrypted communications where appropriate.
  • Controlled access to safeguarding information.
  • Confidential handling of safeguarding records.
  • Periodic security reviews and access monitoring.
  • Compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Further information about how we collect, use, and protect personal information is available in our Privacy Policy.

12. Policy Review

This Safeguarding Policy is reviewed periodically to reflect changes in legislation, safeguarding guidance, and organisational practices.

The latest version will always be published on the Sikh Professionals Network website.
