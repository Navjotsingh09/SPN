Common questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know about SPN Advisors.

Who can become an SPN advisor?

Any experienced Sikh professional who is passionate about giving back can become an SPN Advisor. Whether you work in finance, law, technology, healthcare, or any other sector, if you have knowledge and experience to share, we would love to hear from you.

Do advisors need to commit to regular mentoring?

Advisors are asked to commit to a reasonable level of engagement, but flexibility is built in. Whether you have capacity for one session per month or more, we will work with your schedule to match you with members who will benefit most from your expertise.

Can advisors support faith-related workplace questions?

Absolutely. Many of our advisors have navigated faith-related workplace challenges themselves and are well-placed to offer practical, empathetic guidance on topics such as wearing kirpan, maintaining rehat, requesting religious holidays, and representing Sikh identity in professional environments.

How will members connect with advisors?

Members will be able to connect with advisors through the SPN platform, at network events, and via our scheduled one-to-one and group sessions. We aim to make access easy and accessible, both online and in person across the UK.
