Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Sikh Professionals Network ("SPN", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, join our network, subscribe to our communications, register for events, or otherwise engage with SPN.

Data Controller

Organisation Name: Sikh Professionals Network (SPN)

Registered Parent Entity: Devanhaar

Email: contact@devanhaar.com

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Information You Provide

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • City/location
  • Age or age range
  • Profession
  • Industry sector
  • LinkedIn profile URL
  • Event registration information
  • Membership enquiries
  • Newsletter subscription details
  • Any information submitted through contact forms

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Website usage data
  • Cookie identifiers
  • Pages visited and interaction data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Manage SPN membership enquiries
  • Administer network participation
  • Send newsletters and updates
  • Promote events and activities
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Improve website functionality
  • Maintain network records
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect website security

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we process personal data under one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Consent
  • Contractual necessity
  • Legitimate interests
  • Legal obligations

5. Marketing Communications

Where you have consented, we may send:

  • Event invitations
  • Newsletter updates
  • Community announcements
  • Professional networking opportunities

You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal data.

We may share information with:

  • Website hosting providers
  • CRM and email marketing platforms
  • Event management providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Regulatory authorities where legally required

We collect professional data, including LinkedIn profiles, solely to administer your initial network access. Your profile data will remain strictly confidential and will not be published on any public or member-facing directory until you explicitly opt-in and configure your directory visibility settings in Phase 2.

7. International Data Transfers

Where service providers process data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including UK-approved contractual protections.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for:

  • Membership administration
  • Event management
  • Legal compliance
  • Legitimate business purposes

Data no longer required will be securely deleted.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent

Requests can be submitted via:

Email: contact@devanhaar.com

10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

11. Complaints

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this website.
