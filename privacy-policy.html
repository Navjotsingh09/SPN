Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Sikh Professionals Network ("SPN", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, join our network, subscribe to our communications, register for events, or otherwise engage with SPN.

Data Controller

Organisation Name: Sikh Professionals Network (SPN)
Registered Parent Entity: Devanhaar

Age Eligibility & Safeguarding

Our platform is designed for individuals aged 13 and over in accordance with applicable UK data protection requirements. If you are under 18, we encourage you to read this Privacy Policy with a parent or legal guardian.

We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from children under the age of 13. Where members under the age of 18 participate in SPN networking, mentoring, or community activities, appropriate safeguarding measures will apply in accordance with Devanhaar's safeguarding standards.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Information You Provide

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • City/location
  • Age or age range
  • Profession
  • Industry sector
  • LinkedIn profile URL
  • Event registration information
  • Membership enquiries
  • Newsletter subscription details
  • Any information submitted through contact forms

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Website usage data
  • Cookie identifiers
  • Pages visited and interaction data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Manage SPN membership enquiries
  • Administer network participation
  • Send newsletters and updates
  • Promote events and activities
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Improve website functionality
  • Maintain network records
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect website security

Safeguarding and Mentorship

To protect and safeguard minor participants, where members under the age of 18 engage in our career mentorship, networking, or community initiatives, we may process their contact and participation information under our legal safeguarding obligations.

This helps ensure that interactions are appropriately managed, monitored, secure, and conducted in accordance with Devanhaar's youth safeguarding standards.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we process personal data under one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Consent
  • Contractual necessity
  • Legitimate interests
  • Legal obligations

Standard Personal Data

We process standard professional data, such as your name, email address, sector, profession, and LinkedIn profile, under consent where applicable for communications and contractual necessity where processing is required to manage your membership and provide requested services.

Special Category Data

Membership or participation may reveal religious or philosophical beliefs, which are special category data under the UK GDPR. Where processed, we rely on Article 9(2)(a) of the UK GDPR and obtain explicit, specific, and informed consent separately. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to any other lawful basis.

5. Marketing Communications

Where you have consented, we may send:

  • Event invitations
  • Newsletter updates
  • Community announcements
  • Professional networking opportunities

You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with website hosting providers, CRM and email marketing platforms, event management providers, professional advisers, and regulatory authorities where legally required.

Intra-Group Sharing

Sikh Professionals Network is an initiative owned and operated by Devanhaar. Relevant standard registration and participation data may be shared securely with Devanhaar for safeguarding, professional development, and programme administration, subject to access controls, confidentiality, and applicable data protection requirements.

Professional data, including LinkedIn profiles, is collected solely to administer initial network access and will not be published in a directory until you explicitly opt in and configure visibility settings in Phase 2.

7. International Data Transfers

Where service providers process data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including UK-approved contractual protections.

8. Data Retention

  • Active profile data: Retained while you maintain an active SPN account.
  • Account deletion: Permanently purged within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Inactive accounts: Accounts with no login activity for 2 consecutive years will be flagged for deletion, subject to legal, regulatory, safeguarding, or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Required records will be securely restricted and retained only for the required period.

9. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data, data portability, and withdrawal of consent.

Requests can be submitted via hello@sikhpn.org.

10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access is limited to authorised individuals and service providers with legitimate organisational, administrative, safeguarding, or legal purposes.
