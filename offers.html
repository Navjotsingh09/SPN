SPN Offers

From talks and mentorship to resources, advice, and hands-on experience, SPN gives Sikh professionals across the UK the support they need to grow with confidence while staying true to their identity and values.

What Advisors Offer

1

Talks

SPN organises talks, webinars, seminars, panel discussions, and speaker sessions for Sikh professionals. These help them explore leadership, career growth, and confidence in public service. Members get to learn from pros, chat about hurdles, and boost their confidence in various fields.

2

Resources

SPN makes useful stuff like slide decks, email templates, leaflets, and guidance docs. They help members talk well, know what's expected at work, get ready for chances that come their way, and find support based on Sikh values.

3

Mentorships

SPN offers mentorship to its members looking for job support, career guidance, and professional development. Mentors assist with applications and interviews, provide sector insight, and boost confidence. They guide development and navigating spaces while staying true to Sikh identity.

4

Advice

SPN provides guidance on faith matters, professional conduct, workplace policy, religious accommodation, communication, and community representation. This support helps members approach sensitive or complex situations with clarity, confidence, integrity, and respect.

5

Experience

SPN sets up chances like insight days, work visits, pro shadowing, and cool edu sessions. These help students, grads, and new pros get to know various fields, gain confidence, and pick smart career paths.

Common questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know about what SPN offers.

What kinds of talks and sessions does SPN run?

SPN hosts talks, webinars, seminars, panel discussions, and speaker sessions covering leadership, career growth, and confidence in public and professional life. They give members the chance to learn from experienced professionals and discuss the challenges they face.

Are SPN resources free for members?

Yes. SPN shares slide decks, email templates, leaflets, and guidance documents with members to help them communicate well, understand workplace expectations, and prepare for opportunities, all grounded in Sikh values.

How do I request a mentor through SPN?

Once you join SPN, you can request mentorship for job support, career guidance, and professional development. Mentors help with applications and interviews, share sector insight, and support your growth while staying true to your Sikh identity.

Who can access SPN experience and insight opportunities?

Insight days, work visits, job shadowing, and educational sessions are open to students, graduates, and new professionals. These experiences help members explore different fields, build confidence, and choose a career path that suits them.
