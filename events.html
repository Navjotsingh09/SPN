FAQ

Everything you need to know about SPN events.

Are SPN events online or in person?

SPN runs a mix of in-person events across the UK as well as online webinars and panel discussions, ensuring members from all parts of the country can participate and benefit.

Do I need to be a member to attend events?

Some events are open to the public, while others are exclusive to SPN members. Joining SPN ensures you receive invitations to all upcoming events and have priority access to exclusive sessions.

Can I speak at an SPN event?

Yes. SPN welcomes professionals who want to share their knowledge and experience. If you are interested in speaking or moderating at an event, please register your interest using the form above.

What types of events will SPN run?

SPN hosts a wide range of events including networking evenings, career webinars, panel discussions, Parliament Day, professional insight days, and the annual SPN Awards — all designed to connect, empower, and inspire.
