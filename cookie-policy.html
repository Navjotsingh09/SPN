Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They help websites function correctly, improve performance, and provide analytical insights.

2. How SPN Uses Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Enable website functionality
  • Remember preferences
  • Measure website performance
  • Improve user experience
  • Analyse visitor behaviour
  • Support marketing activities where consent has been provided

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for website functionality.

Examples:

  • Security cookies
  • Session management cookies
  • Form submission cookies

These cannot be disabled through our cookie banner.

Analytics Cookies

Used to understand website usage.

Examples:

  • Google Analytics
  • Visitor statistics
  • Traffic reporting

These are only activated with your consent.

Functional Cookies

Used to remember preferences such as:

  • Language settings
  • User preferences
  • Form preferences

Marketing Cookies

May be used in future to:

  • Measure campaign performance
  • Track engagement
  • Improve event promotion effectiveness

These require explicit consent.

4. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie banner allowing you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Manage cookie preferences

You may update your preferences at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Mailchimp or similar email marketing platforms
  • Event registration platforms
  • Social media integrations

These providers may place cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Manage cookie settings

Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact website functionality.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
