Cookie Policy

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar tracking technologies are used on the Sikh Professionals Network ("SPN") website, which is owned and operated by Devanhaar ("we", "us", "our").

Cookies help us provide a secure and reliable website, improve functionality, analyse website performance, remember your preferences, and enhance your browsing experience. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

For more information about how we process personal information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enable essential website functionality.
  • Remember your cookie preferences.
  • Secure our website and forms.
  • Analyse website traffic and visitor behaviour.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Process form submission metadata.
  • Monitor website security and performance.
  • Support marketing and communications where consent has been provided.

Cookies and tracking technologies may process limited technical information including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Website usage statistics
  • Form submission metadata
  • Network registration inputs where forms are submitted

3. Types of Cookies Used

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation and security of our website and cannot be disabled through our cookie banner. They include cookies used for:

  • Website security
  • Session management
  • Form submission processing
  • Load balancing
  • Remembering your cookie consent preferences

4. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie banner allowing you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Manage cookie preferences

You may update your preferences at any time.

5. Third-Party Technologies

Our website may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Google Tag Manager
  • Google Analytics
  • Web3Forms (secure form processing)
  • Next.js website framework
  • Email marketing platforms
  • Event registration platforms
  • Social media integrations

These services may place cookies or process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Managing Your Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Control cookie permissions

You can also remove cookies using the browser settings below:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete Browsing Data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy, Search and Services → Clear Browsing Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality and security of certain areas of our website.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
