Common questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know about SPN and getting involved.

What does it mean to join the SPN Network?

Joining the SPN Network means becoming part of a growing UK-wide community of Sikh professionals, graduates and emerging leaders. You gain access to mentoring, community events, member spotlights and networking opportunities — all grounded in shared identity, professional excellence and the spirit of seva.

Will there be a member directory?

Yes. We are building a secure professional directory where members across the United Kingdom can find and connect with one another. It is currently in development and will be made available to members as the network grows.

Will my personal details be public?

No. Your personal details are kept private and are never shared publicly. Any future directory will be members-only and built with your privacy and consent in mind — you choose what information you share with the community.

Can I join if I am still studying?

Absolutely. SPN is open to anyone aged 18 and over, including students and recent graduates. Whether you are still studying or just starting your career, you are welcome to join and grow alongside the network.
